The CRS loop

Four steps from "I need research" to "delivered"

  1. 01

    Experts apply

    Researchers submit their specialism, rate, and experience. Nothing goes live yet.

  2. 02

    Admin verifies

    Every application is reviewed and approved before an expert can be hired. The gold seal means vetted.

  3. 03

    You hire

    Compare ratings and reviews, pick your expert, and send a project request describing your scope.

  4. 04

    Pay on completion

    When the work is delivered, release payment through the platform. You get a receipt; they get paid.

Heads up: payments on this demo are simulated — no real money moves. It's the full workflow, safely fake.
